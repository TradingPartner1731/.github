# 1731 Partner

**Two former roommates building trading bots in the Bay Area.**

We work at tech companies and came to the markets from outside traditional finance. Together, we're exploring how software, systematic research, and AI agents can help us build better tools for stocks and options trading.

The name **1731** comes from our former home address in San Jose, where our partnership began.

## What we're building

- **Trading bots:** turning strategy ideas into systems we can test, monitor, and improve.
- **Research tools:** working with market data, backtests, and measured results.
- **Agent workflows:** exploring where AI agents can help with research, analysis, and trading operations.

We're learning in public where we can, with an engineering mindset and respect for market risk. Our work here is exploratory and is not financial advice.
